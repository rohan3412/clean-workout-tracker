# What's new in Clean

One section per version, newest first, as `## <versionName> (<date>)`, then one `- Title: sentence` line per change. The app
shows the section of the version it is once after an update, as a page: the first six changes are its highlights (a picture chosen by the title's first word, see `highlightIcon`), the rest and every earlier version are under "See everything" and in Settings, About.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-25)
- Look: Themes in Dark or Light, each with its own background (grid, dots, pluses, waves, grain or paper) behind frosted bars and cards, and one accent colour for what matters.
- Cards open into pages: Tap a card and it grows into its page. Back shrinks it home, and the numbers and charts travel with it.
- Hold to peek: Press and hold a day, a workout, a bar or a muscle to see what is behind it, then let go.
- Logging: A number pad of its own, a rest bar you can swipe away, Undo for what you remove, and a light buzz as each set is done.
- Progress: Trends, personal bests, trophies, a PR timeline and a year in review, all close to hand.
- Photos: Progress photos and snapshots are one Photos section on Profile: take one in the app or choose one, with filters, labels, Compare and an optional blur and lock.
- Performance: Settings, Sound and feel, Performance trims blur and animation for slower phones, in three steps.
- Settings: Nine short pages, with chips that jump to a section and light its heading, and a search you pull down.
- Backup: A Back up now button beside the backup folder, and the last backup shown as minutes, hours or days ago.
- Swipes: Moving between tabs follows your finger and carries on at its speed, and the dock's highlight goes with it.
- Home: Up next, the week's ring, your weight line, today's steps from Health Connect if you switch it on, and your last workout.
- Activity: One calendar of every training day, scrolling back to your first.
- Body: The figure shows what you trained today; hold a muscle for a closer look.
- Records: Every PR is the accent colour, and each exercise has a PR history you can scroll through.
- Search: Pull the Library or History down, or tap the search icon, to find things quickly.
- Finishing: A summary after each workout with your records and badges, and a card to share.
- Buzzes: One small set of haptics everywhere: a tick for choosing, a firm one for done, a heavy one for a record.
- Readable colours: Text and charts were checked in every theme, and colours that mean something never sit close to your accent.
- Colour-blind safe: A switch trades green and red for blue and orange.

## 1.0.3
- Cards open into their pages: A tapped card grows into its page, and what is on both, like the body figure, moves to its new place. Back shrinks the page into the card again.

## 1.0.2
- Source code: Clean's code is a tap away under Settings, Credits.
- What's new: After each update this card shows what changed. Earlier versions are under Settings, Credits.
- Adding an exercise: The exercise list opens as a card over your workout, so you never lose your place.
- Light look: On the Strength map, Full is now a deep burnt orange that sits on the same scale as the rest.
- Settings search: Results line up with the search box instead of touching the edge of the screen.
