# What Clean is

The yardstick for every design decision. Where a screen, a mockup or a rule elsewhere disagrees with this page, this page
wins until the user changes it (set down 2026-11-18, from the user's words).

## In one line

**A useful, frictionless workout tracker for people who train seriously.**

## Who it is for

Someone who lifts several times a week, for years, and keeps a precise record: weights to the half kilo, variations kept
apart, left and right sides, RPE, PRs that mean something. They open Clean between sets with chalk on their hands and a
heart rate of 150. They know what they are doing; the app does not teach, cheer or sell. It keeps the record and makes it
easy to read.

## The job, in order

1. **Logging a set is instant.** Fewest taps, nothing to read, nothing that moves unless it was touched. This is the
   screen used most and the one judged hardest.
2. **The record is trustworthy.** Numbers are exact, nothing is rounded away, everything can be checked by tapping it.
3. **Progress is legible at a glance.** Home answers "what next, am I consistent, am I getting stronger, what do I weigh"
   without a tap.
4. **Everything deeper is one tap away**, never in the way.

## The feeling

**Calm and focused while lifting, quietly proud when finished.** The app is a well-made tool, like a good barbell: plain
until you use it, then it feels exactly right in the hand.

## Principles

1. **Simplicity first, in the places used under load.** The workout screen, the number pad and Home's top are as plain as
   they can be. Simplicity is not minimalism: what a serious lifter checks often stays close (Apple, Simplicity: "keeps
   the important things close by and lets the others fall away").
2. **Delight lives in interaction, not decoration.** A screen at rest is quiet: no glows, no gradients for their own sake,
   no motion that was not asked for. Delight is in how things respond: a tick that settles with a light buzz, a number that
   rolls to its new value, a PR that lands with one small shine, a card that grows into its page, a rest that ends with the
   right sound. Feel first, look second (Apple, Delight: "Don't mistake delight for decoration").
3. **Colour means something.** One accent, used for the primary action, selection, the running workout and the data
   (calendar, charts, body figure). There are twelve themes (round 34: Graphite, Slate, Crimson, Ember, Saffron, Green, Teal, Lagoon, Cobalt, Iris, Orchid, Rose), each designed separately for Dark and for Light. A PR has a colour of its own per theme (round 34: a gold, or lemon in Ember, mint in Saffron, apricot in Green), never the accent, amber a warm-up or a note, red a deletion; green and its neighbours are never borrowed for the accent's own jobs, and no meaning colour sits within 36 degrees of hue of the theme's accent. Everything else is neutral.
4. **One signature, spent in one place.** Clean's signature is the **activity calendar**: the record of every training day,
   scrollable back to the first one. It is where colour lives at full strength; around it everything stays quiet.
5. **Depth from light and material, not lines and boxes.** Floating things (tab bar, plus, sheets, number pad, rest panel)
   are a frosted material; content sits on the page or on one raised step. No box inside a box.
6. **Numbers are the content.** Large, tabular, exact, with units; labels are small and grey. A number can always be tapped
   to see where it came from.
7. **Never lose a good feature to a redesign.** A redesign changes how things look and where they sit, not what the app
   can do. Anything removed is removed on purpose, by the user's word.
8. **Familiar, then distinctive.** Navigation and controls behave like Android's; identity lives in the material, the
   type of the numbers, the calendar and the moments.

## Levels of expression

How much of the look is turned up is a dial, not a style. A design states its level:

- **Quiet**: neutral ground, solid surfaces, accent only on action and data. Delight only through motion and haptics.
- **Lit**: Quiet plus the frosted material on what floats, lit edges, and one faint light of the accent behind Home's top.
- **Glow**: Lit plus a stronger ambient light, the accent's soft gradient on the primary button, a glow only on a PR or a
  streak.

Above Glow is decoration (round 1 of the 1.1 remake, "Aurora", "Heat", "Prism", was judged too bold) and is out of
bounds.

## What Clean is not

Not a coach, a social feed, a game or a store. No streak guilt, no confetti for opening the app, no upsell, no account,
no internet.
