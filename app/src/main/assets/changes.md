# What's new in Clean

One section per version, newest first, as `## <versionName> (<date>)`, then one `- Title: sentence` line per change. The app
shows the section of the version it is once after an update (one swipe per change) and every section under Settings, Credits.

## 1.0.4
- Moving around: Every card and row grows into the page it opens, and what is on both travels to its new place.
- Workout: The next-set highlight glides, a done set's colour sweeps across from the tick, and a finished exercise folds into its line.
- Removing and undoing: Removed sets and exercises close the gap smoothly, and Undo glides them back.
- Change type: Tapping a set's number grows its menu out of it, with the set kept lit.
- Reordering: Holding an exercise gathers the others around it, and it stays exactly where your finger is.
- Number pad: Touching anywhere outside it puts it away.
- Numbers and charts: Numbers roll to their new value, and switching a chart's range stretches the line into place.
- Week ring: The ring and the day marks fill from where they were.
- Tabs: The tab pill and the exercise page's underline stretch from tab to tab and follow your swipe.
- Rewards: A new PR badge lands with a shine, and a new badge flips in like a coin.
- Weight ruler: Flick it and it coasts, then snaps to the nearest step with a light tick on each whole number.
- Calendar: The full calendar ends at today, and fills in a wave the first time you open it.
- Performance mode: Settings, Appearance turns every animation off.
- Smoother moves: Names and numbers keep their shape while they travel and change only mid-way, so nothing jumps at the start or end.
- History cards: A workout's date, totals, PRs and exercises travel into its page, and the Best set column is gone.
- Body figure: Swiping brings the new colours in from the side of the card.

## 1.0.3
- Cards open into their pages: A tapped card grows into its page, and what is on both, like the body figure, moves to its new place. Back shrinks the page into the card again.

## 1.0.2
- Source code: Clean's code is a tap away under Settings, Credits.
- What's new: After each update this card shows what changed. Earlier versions are under Settings, Credits.
- Adding an exercise: The exercise list opens as a card over your workout, so you never lose your place.
- Light look: On the Strength map, Full is now a deep burnt orange that sits on the same scale as the rest.
- Settings search: Results line up with the search box instead of touching the edge of the screen.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-15)
- First release on GitHub: One APK for each kind of phone, from the public code.
